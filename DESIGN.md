## Brand & Style

This design system models an ultra-high precision remote sensing desktop environment built atop a warm, tangible surface. It combines physical laboratory desk metaphors with the sharp, rigorous instrumentation of modern satellite intelligence workstations.

### Aesthetic Tone & Personality
- **Materiality & Focus:** Evokes the tactile sensation of field notebooks, heavy-stock cartographic prints, and milled anodized instruments resting on a natural birch or sand workbench.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision:** Dense with telemetry, coordinates, and real-time active fire vectors, yet structured to eliminate optical fatigue during hours of geospatial surveillance.
- **Utilitarian Warmth:** eschews cold, sterile dashboard grays in favor of balanced earth tones, paper backdrops, and mechanical controls reminiscent of field-ready scientific apparatus.

### Visual Architecture
The canvas acts as a physical drafting table: a warm sandy ground layer upon which resting app windows, telemetry sheets, and dockable viewport modules sit. Layering uses crisp 1px borders, subtle debossed grooves, and microscopic drop shadows that simulate real paper sheets sitting in crisp laboratory light.

## Colors

The palette balances earthy background substrates with razor-sharp functional pigments calibrated for rapid visual triage of thermal radiation anomalies and telemetry states.

### Substrates & Surfaces
- **Workbench Canvas (`#E1D7C2`):** The foundational tactile desk plane behind floating workspace windows.
- **Sheet Paper Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity focal panels, active telemetry windows, and data tables.
- **Cream Paper Secondary (`#FDFDF8`):** Toolbars, contextual sidebars, and inactive inspect panels.
- **Subsurface Container (`#F4F3ED`):** Embedded inputs, sunken cell containers, and recessed graph backdrops.

### Ink & Contrast Typography
- **Deep Moss (`#23251D`):** Primary ink for critical metrics, system headers, and active high-contrast labels.
- **Olive Char (`#4D4F46`):** Secondary structural copy, inactive tab labels, and axis markers.
- **Sage Gray (`#65675E`):** Tertiary data hints, unit annotations, and timestamp prefixes.

### Structural Seams & Division
- **Faded Pewter (`#B3B3AF`):** Outer perimeter stroke for principal workstation windows.
- **Warm Mist (`#BFC1B7`):** Primary 1px interior dividers, pane partitions, and container borders.
- **Ash Green (`#9EA096`):** Focused states, active pane splitters, and deliberate metric separators.

### Functional Accents & Thermal Tiers
- **Amber Glow (`#F0A000`):** VIIRS detection confidence markers, primary commit triggers, active sensor calibrators.
- **Flame Orange (`#F54E00`):** Real-time thermal anomalies, critical hazard perimeters, fire alerts.
- **Signal Blue (`#2F80FA`):** Interactive coordinates, selection anchors, active spatial polygons.
- **Moss Green (`#6AA84F`):** Satellite uplink verification, valid ingestion loops, nominal station health.

## Typography

The typography couples geometric, rounded-character humanism for system-level controls with cold, calibrated monospacing for raw satellite vectors.

### Role Hierarchy & Application
- **Headers & Analytical Prose (`Inter`):** Renders operational summaries, dashboard module titles, and modal headers. Uses tighter negative tracking at larger scales to maintain typographic tension.
- **Instrumentation & Control Labels (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Serves all field names, column headers, button labels, and status badges. Small sizes (10-11px) leverage expanded letter spacing to ensure immediate scannability under emergency triage.
- **Coordinates & Spatial Feed (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to telemetry readouts, latitude/longitude pairings, spectral band numbers (e.g. Band 7/VIIRS 375m), and epoch timestamps. Monospace sizing aligns cleanly to fractional data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The workstation layout uses a bounded container philosophy where floating workspace windows frame internal multi-pane instruments.

### Workspace Frame
- The desktop canvas displays an outer margin of `margin` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) over the Sandy Desk plane, creating a perimeter rim around the primary active application viewport.
- Split-screen panels (e.g. Map Projection Canvas alongside Orthorectified Thermal Feed and Anomaly Log) dock with `gutter-dense` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) gutters to maximize spatial density without visual collision.

### Component Internal Metrics
- **Dense Scientific Padding:** Field tools and table rows conform to a strict 4px baseline rhythm. Interactive button heights, filter pill paddings, and header heights stay compact (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal).
- **Reflow Architecture:** At display widths below 1280px, auxiliary inspector rails collapse into drawer drawers; below 960px, the geospatial map converts to full-canvas presentation with floating bottom sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is modeled as stacked sheets of fine rag paper and milled aluminum framing resting under directional midday sunlight.

### Shadow Architecture
- **Desk Level 0 (Recessed Slots & Field Inputs):**
  Inner shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(35, 37, 29, 0.08)`. Simulates cutouts into the cream card stock.
- **Desk Level 1 (Flat Panes & Document Sheets):**
  Border: `1px solid #BFC1B7`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(35, 37, 29, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(35, 37, 29, 0.03)`. Applied to side panels and analytical widgets.
- **Desk Level 2 (Main Floating Workstation Window):**
  Border: `1px solid #B3B3AF`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(50, 48, 40, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(50, 48, 40, 0.04)`. Establishes the physical separation of the workstation platform from the sand background.
- **Desk Level 3 (Context Menus, Inspectors & Overlays):**
  Border: `1px solid #9EA096`. Shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(35, 37, 29, 0.18), 0 6px 12px -3px rgba(35, 37, 29, 0.08)`.

### Layered Separation
Surfaces rely on structural `1px` hairpins rather than large blurs. Panes abut using thin hairline borders in `#BFC1B7` to provide the disciplined cadence of drafting software.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects tight mechanical production tolerances: subtle, controlled rounding that prevents harsh visual snags while maximizing interior pixel real estate.

### Radius Specifications
- **App Windows & Outer Modules:** `4px` (`rounded-sm`). Mimics clean-die-cut paperboard pads.
- **Controls, Action Pills, and Inputs:** `3px` to `4px`.
- **Badges, Sensor Telemetry Chips, and Coordinates:** `2px` to preserve a crisp, technical stamp aesthetic.
- **Thermal Map Markers & Precision Plot Nodes:** `50%` (Circular) with `1.5px` high-contrast outer rings.

## Components

### Buttons & Hardware Triggers
- **Primary / VIIRS Action (`Amber Glow`):** `#F0A000` solid fill, `#23251D` text label, `1px solid #C78500` bottom rim shadow. Active state depresses `1px` downward with inset micro-shadow.
- **Hazard Trigger (`Flame Orange`):** `#F54E00` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, `1px solid #C43E00` edge. Used solely for anomaly escalations and live tracking triggers.
- **Tactile Document Button (Neutral Standard):** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#4D4F46` label, `1px solid #BFC1B7` border, `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`. On hover: `#FDFDF8` background, `#23251D` text, border shifts to `#9EA096`.

### Inputs, Search & Sensor Sliders
- **Telemetry Input Fields:** `#F4F3ED` recessed background, `1px solid #BFC1B7` border, `JetBrains Mono` text (`#23251D`). Focused state renders a crisp `1px solid #2F80FA` outline with zero fuzzy ring spreads.
- **Coordinate Steppers:** Dual-button segmented inputs with centered monospace values, separated by hairline `#BFC1B7` vertical dividers.

### Data Chips & Telemetry Status Tags
- **Uplink Nominal:** `#FDFDF8` pill, `1px solid #6AA84F` hairline outline, text `#4D4F46`, featuring a `6px` solid `#6AA84F` pulse indicator dot.
- **Active Thermal Hazard Pill:** `#F54E00` background at `12%` opacity, `1px solid #F54E00`, text `#F54E00`, displaying radiative power values (`MW`) in bold monospace.
- **Spectral Band Identifier:** Solid `#23251D` badge with crisp white monospace text (`B07`, `B12`).

### Data Tables & Log Readouts
- **Table Construction:** Zero vertical internal borders. Horizontal borders use `1px solid #F4F3ED`. Row hover shifts subtly to `#F8F8F2`.
- **Header Cells:** `#FDFDF8` surface, `10px` uppercase `IBM Plex Sans` tracking `+0.05em`, text color `#65675E`. Sticky on scroll.

### Workstation Cards & Floating Inspector Panes
- **Paper Inspector Card:** Built on `#FFFFFF` with `#FDFDF8` titlebar. The titlebar features a `24px` height profile, uppercase technical labeling, an inline status LED, and flat close/minimize glyphs. Bottom card footer houses monospaced timestamp stamps (`UTC`).
